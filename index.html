STEP 01 · FIELD RESEARCH

Territory Pitch

The starting point: a territory, a tension, and the questions that will guide the inquiry.

TERRITORY TITLE

AI-Assisted Effects for Sports Video Editing

ONE-SENTENCE CURIOSITY STATEMENT

How can AI help sports videographers make unique editing effects and transitions while making the editing process easier but keeping the creativity.

SHORT PARAGRAPH

I want to see how AI can improve the process of editing sports videos and help create more unique looking content. I would want to make effects, transitions, animations, color grading/editing techniques. That could hopefully transfer over to work in Adobe Premiere and After Effects.

Editing videos can be so time consuming and sometimes when you get into a funk and can't figure out what you are trying to add to an edit it would be nice to be able to search to find an effect or ask it to make you one when you can't think of it. I want to see how it can be an assistant by providing ideas, tutorials and techniques that help bring creative visions to life.

STEP 02 · COMMAND CENTER

Inquiry Brief

Sharpening the question, naming the stakes, and defining what needs to be learned.

RESEARCH QUESTION

How can AI help sports editors by receiving step-by-step tutorials on how to edit certain effects in editing software?

WHY IT MATTERS

AI can help generate things quickly which can be very resourceful when editing. It is easy to get stuck on one thing you are trying to accomplish and if you are in a time crunch sometimes you can't spend the time on things that you want to do.

Having AI there to tell you how to do something that you are trying to figure out can help make editing more efficient and less stressful.

WHAT I NEED TO LEARN
  1. How AI can provide steps to create something and it actually be accurate
  2. How AI can provide steps that transfer over to a certain editing software
  3. How well the AI can understand the effects that someone is asking for and how well they can explain it
POSSIBLE FINAL OUTCOME

An AI assistance tool to give step-by-step tutorials on how to create an effect and help create ideas. Two concrete directions: step-by-step tutorials for creating an effect, and an AI generated search to help map out and create ideas.

STEP 03 · RESEARCH MAP

Sources & Notes

Four external sources mapped against the inquiry, with key findings captured in full.

01Online article

Bringing generative AI to video editing workflows in Adobe Premiere Pro

Adobe

Adobe's article explains how generative AI can improve video editing in Premiere Pro by saving time and making editing more efficient. AI tools can extend video clips, add or remove objects, and generate new footage using text prompts. Adobe also plans to integrate different AI models, giving editors more creative options without needing to switch between programs. These features are designed to automate repetitive tasks while allowing editors to focus more on creativity and storytelling. Overall, Adobe believes AI should assist video editors rather than replace them, making the editing process faster and more flexible.

Open source
02Online article

Demo2Tutorial: From Human Experience to Multimodal Software Tutorials

Bai et al. · CVPR 2026

Explains how AI can turn screen recordings and computer interactions into easy-to-follow, step-by-step tutorials. The technology analyzes a person's actions, identifies important steps, and creates instructions using images and text to explain how to complete tasks. Researchers found that these AI-generated tutorials helped people complete tasks faster and improved the ability of AI agents to perform computer-based tasks. The study also found that the generated tutorials could be more effective than traditional human-made tutorials. Overall, the research shows how AI can make learning software easier, improve instructional content, and help people become more efficient when using digital tools.

Open source
03Online article

AI Video Effects for Sports Clips: Meet Rover AI Master Repaint

BleeqUp

Explains how BleeqUp's Rover AI Master Repaint feature uses artificial intelligence to transform ordinary sports footage into more creative and visually interesting videos. The tool allows editors to apply different visual styles and effects to selected clips, making them more engaging for social media posts and sports recaps. It works alongside AI editing tools that help capture and select important moments, giving creators more options without needing to record new footage. However, the article emphasizes that original footage should be kept for accurate sports analysis, training, and safety documentation because AI-edited footage may change the appearance of what actually happened. Overall, the article shows how AI can help sports editors create more unique and visually appealing content while saving time and expanding their creative possibilities.

Open source
04Online article

SportsBuddy: Designing and Evaluating an AI-Powered Sports Video Storytelling Tool Through Real-World Deployment

Harvard SEAS · VCG

Explains how SportsBuddy uses artificial intelligence to make sports video editing and storytelling easier for coaches, athletes, content creators, and fans. The tool combines AI player tracking, interactive features, and timeline visualizations to help users create game highlights and explain important moments without needing advanced video editing skills. Researchers found that more than 150 sports users had used SportsBuddy to create highlights, and feedback showed that it made video storytelling easier and more accessible. The study also demonstrated how the technology can improve coaching communication, game analysis, and fan engagement. Overall, the research shows how AI can simplify sports video editing while helping people better understand and communicate important moments in games.

NOTES / EXPERIMENT LOG

Experiment Log

Four structured tests with ChatGPT, each testing whether AI can teach a real Premiere Pro technique.

TEST 01 Worked well
SOURCE

ChatGPT

INTERACTION

“Can you teach me how to make a light leak effect for sports editing, give me a step by step tutorial on how to do it in adobe premiere”

FINDINGS

It actually worked very well — it provided two different ways to create a light leak and the directions it gave me were very simple and effective.

TEST 02 Partial result
SOURCE

ChatGPT

INTERACTION

“Can you teach me how to make a masking effect for sports editing, give me a step by step tutorial on how to do it in adobe premiere”

FINDINGS

The directions it gave me for the masking worked very well, but it wanted me to add a cool text masking effect and the text instructions did not work — the directions it describes make the text disappear; it doesn't appear behind the masks like it says to.

TEST 03 Needed more input
SOURCE

ChatGPT

INTERACTION

“Can you teach me how to color grade a video for sports editing that has a red football field, give me a step by step tutorial on how to do it in adobe premiere”

FINDINGS

I appreciated the directions of the color grading but the color grading itself wasn't the best. I provided a still image of the colors of the video, but I think it needed more information like if I shot in S-Log and what type of camera I shot on. The directions were explained well but some parts are not detailed enough.

TEST 03 · PT.2 Follow-up
SOURCE

ChatGPT

INTERACTION

“My camera is Sony. My color profile is S-Log / Log. My desired look is Cinematic and dramatic. Please build my custom Premiere Pro color grading settings for my red football field footage. I shot in PP9 and I have a A7III”

FINDINGS

A follow-up to Test 03 that supplies the missing camera data (Sony A7III, S-Log, PP9) identified in the previous test. Findings were not recorded in the source notes; the resulting captures are preserved in Reference Images.

PATTERN EMERGING

What the tests suggest

01

Simple, well-known effects are explained accurately and usefully.

02

Layered effects (mask + text) fail — instructions can produce the opposite result.

03

Context is everything: camera model, picture profile, and software version dramatically change output quality.

DESIGN SPECIFICATION

Input templates

TEMPLATE FOR PEOPLE TO FILL OUT
  1. What kind of camera is being used
  2. What were the settings of the camera when it was being used
  3. What editing software they're using
  4. What type of sport they filmed
  5. What effects they want to learn or implement
  6. If anything they want generated (insert detailed description)
GENERATIVE TEMPLATE / SPECIFICS
  1. Detailed description of idea
  2. Provide color (if any)
  3. Provide direction of movement (if any)
  4. Use keywords that are similar to an effect it already knows (see Effects Library)

Then AI can create a map out of what you want and need and take you through it — kind of like a lesson plan.
